\section{Selection procedure}
\label{sec:procedure}

Given room ($V_{\mathrm{room}}$, $L_{\max}$), band top $f_2$,
peak-equivalent target $L_t$, distortion budgets $D^\ast$ (HD) and
$D^\ast_{\mathrm{IM}}$ (Doppler), damping target $\Qtc^\ast$ (e.g.\ $0.55$),
burst factor $C$ (\cref{lem:shape}, take 2), burst headroom $\kappa$:

\begin{enumerate}
	\item[0.] \textbf{Room closure:} 
		$f_{\mathrm{pz}}=c/(2L_{\max})$; band bottom 
		$f_1\to f_{\mathrm{pz}}$; $\xi^\ast=D^\ast/0.1$ (or $\sqrt{D^\ast/0.1}$
		if odd-order dominated).
	\item \textbf{Displacement/distortion:} 
		$\Vd\ge$~\eqref{eq:Vdreq}; predicted 
		$D\approx d_2\xi_x+d_3\xi_x^2$ \eqref{eq:HDscale}.
	\item \textbf{Dissipation:} 
		$\eta_0\Pmax\ge10^{(L_t-112.2)/10}$; thermal AM
		$\approx0.034\,\xi_P\Delta T_{\mathrm{rated}}$\,dB.
	\item \textbf{Split (Doppler):} 
		choose $f_{\mathrm{sp}}$ with $\pi 
		f_{\mathrm{sp}}X_1^{\max}/c\le D^\ast_{\mathrm{IM}}$ \eqref{eq:doppler}.
	\item \textbf{Corner/settling/box:} 
		$\Qts\le\Qtc^\ast$;
		$F_s/\Qts\simeq f_{\mathrm{pz}}/\Qtc^\ast$ (sub) resp.\ $F_c\lesssim
		f_{\mathrm{sp}}$ (attack); 
		$\Vb=\Vas/((\Qtc^\ast/\Qts)^2-1)$; 
		box distortion \eqref{eq:boxHD} within budget.
	\item \textbf{Regime map:} 
		verify $f_x$ \eqref{eq:fx} and $p_{\max}(\omega)$
		\eqref{eq:master} $\ge$ target across the band.
	\item \textbf{Validity:} 
		$f_2\ll f_L$; $ka(f_2)\lesssim1$; 
		consistent $\Xmax$/power-rating standards across candidates.
\end{enumerate}

Ranking within one role: lexicographic on predicted non-correctable
distortion (\cref{prop:equivdist}) --- sub role maximizes $\Vd$, tie-break
on $\Sd$ and $\eta_0\Pmax$; attack role maximizes $\eta_0\Pmax$ subject to
its own utilization and Doppler index, tie-break on $f_L$ and shorting
rings.
