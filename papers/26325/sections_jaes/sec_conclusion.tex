\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

For the architecture declared in \cref{sec:intro-arch}---a compact prepared
listening area, half-space soffit mounting, a mono low-bass manifold of
mechanically opposed pairs, independent stereo upper-bass channels, and
finite-band DSP alignment---this report assembles established sealed-box
relations into a reproducible pre-purchase ranking procedure that operates
on public datasheet fields.

\textbf{What is exact within the model.} The sealed-box equation, the
alignment line \cref{eq:alignment}, the displacement- and
dissipation-limited ceilings \cref{eq:SPLL,eq:SPLT}, their crossover
\cref{eq:fx}, and the steady electrical requirements
\cref{eq:Ereq,eq:Ireq,eq:Preq} follow from the stated lumped model. The
transient factor \cref{eq:ctr} is obtained numerically from the same model
for a declared waveform family, which removes the invalid constant burst
factor near $F_c$.

\textbf{What is empirical.} In the frozen candidate population analysed
here, and under the canonical scenario, the well-ranked low-bass and
upper-bass candidates occupy different regions of the descriptor plane
(\cref{fig:database-pareto,tab:database-summary}). This is an observation
about an available population, not a proof that one driver cannot serve
both roles.

\textbf{What is policy.} The hard gates, the choice of core Pareto
objectives, the $0.80$ preferred excursion margin, the Doppler sideband
budget, the $\Qtc$ ceiling, the enclosure limit, and the role-specific
lexicographic orders are declared engineering choices. Their influence is
measured, not assumed, by the robustness analysis of
\cref{sec:procedure-robustness}.

\textbf{What is application-specific.} The worked system of
\cref{app:example} meets its declared targets within every gate, including
the finite per-driver amplifier limits, for the stated room, targets, and
bands. It is a result for that scenario, not a general recommendation.

\textbf{Limitations.} The model is small-signal and LTI; nonlinear
indicators are proxies, not predicted distortion; $\Xmax$, $\Pmax$, and
$\Le$ conventions differ between manufacturers; the room model is a
low-order description of a prepared room over one band and one small area;
DSP correction is finite-band and does not address nonlinear residuals; and
no controlled subjective evaluation is reported. Most importantly, the
robustness analysis validates the \emph{stability} of the decision under
uncertain inputs, not the physical correctness of the nonlinear indicators.
The natural next steps are a public large-signal measurement subset for
proxy validation, a measured room transfer over the couch area in place of
the analytical model, and measurement of a built system against the
predicted gates.
