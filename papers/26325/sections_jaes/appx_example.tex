\section{Worked example}
\label{app:example}

Representative (rounded) parameters for two real driver classes --- S, a
long-throw 18'' subwoofer, and M, a high-efficiency 12'' mid-bass unit ---
illustrate the chain end to end. Every derived number is produced by the
open-source reference implementation \cite{audioshape-preprint} from the
datasheet rows alone.

Against target $L_t=110$\,dB (1\,m, $2\pi$, rms): S meets 110\,dB down to
$22.7$\,Hz on sine, needing only 141\,W thermally ($\ll600$\,W rated) ---
nowhere near thermally limited at this target. S cannot serve as attack
driver: $f_L=139$\,Hz sits inside the attack band, a $\Qtc=0.55$ alignment
needs 918\,L, and its corner rate ($42$\,Hz) cannot be raised
(\cref{prop:invariance}). M holds 117\,dB sine at 80\,Hz, but its
\emph{transient} ceiling at 70\,Hz clips excursion at a 110\,dB kick,
fixing the split at $\ge$80\,Hz --- set by the burst penalty
(\cref{lem:shape}), not the sine math. Both drivers saturate the materials
bound at $\beta\approx0.3$ (\cref{thm:bound}): two ends of one Pareto front.

\subsection*{Distortion-referenced sizing with room closure}

Room $V_{\mathrm{room}}=60$\,m$^3$, $L_{\max}=6$\,m
$\Rightarrow f_{\mathrm{pz}}=28.6$\,Hz; target 110\,dB at the listening
position $r_{\mathrm{listen}}=3$\,m; split $f_{\mathrm{sp}}=80$\,Hz;
pressure-zone demand $V_{\mathrm{dem}}=4.36$\,L, flat for
$f\le f_{\mathrm{pz}}$.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{../figures/fig_distortion_sub}
\caption{Driver S: motor/suspension HD, Doppler IM onto 80\,Hz, box
air-spring HD2, and their sum, at the 110\,dB target
($r_{\mathrm{listen}}=3$~m).}
\label{fig:distortion-sub}
\end{figure}

One S gives utilization $\xi_x=4.36/2.30=1.89$: infeasible even before
distortion. Two S: $\xi_x=0.95\Rightarrow D\approx9.0$--$9.5\%$. At budget
$D^\ast=3\%$, $\xi^\ast=0.3$--$0.55$ requires $7.95$--$14.52$\,L, i.e.\
\textbf{4--7 units of S}: the distortion budget, not the SPL ceiling,
multiplies driver count by $2$--$3.5\times$. Doppler: two S carrying their
own band to 80\,Hz costs $1.4\%$ IM, versus $4.3\%$ for a hypothetical
single full-range cone covering to 250\,Hz; the split buys $\sim3\times$
lower FM distortion. Box spring: two S in 473\,L each gives
$\mathrm{HD}_2^{\mathrm{box}}\approx0.11\%$, roughly two orders below the
motor terms. Thermal: $\xi_P\approx3.8\%$, still comfortably small next to
the driver's rating. The $F_s$ rule requires $F_s/\Qts\le52$\,Hz; S has
42\,Hz --- compliant, with $F_c=25.5$\,Hz undershooting $f_{\mathrm{pz}}$ by
$3.1$\,Hz at $\Qtc=0.61$ ($M_p\le1\%$, \cref{prop:decay}): a $0.99$\,dB
in-room shelf, trimmed by a free EQ cut rather than chasing exact alignment
at a less comfortable $\Qtc$.
